Privacy policy

This policy explains what information Oxybricks.world collects, why we use it, and the choices available to you when you use our platform.

01

Introduction and applicability

  • This policy describes the information we may collect from you, or that you may provide through the platform, and how that information is collected, used, processed, disclosed and maintained.
  • It applies to information you provide on the platform, including information you upload, emails you exchange with us or other users, and other information submitted to us.
  • It does not apply to information collected by third parties through the platform, links on the platform, or advertisements. Those parties are responsible for their own privacy practices.

02

Information we collect

  • We may collect information directly from you or through third parties. This can include personally identifiable information, usage patterns, searches, advertisements or third-party links you visit, and correspondence with Oxybricks.world.
  • Usage details may include your IP address, device and browser information, location, connections, cookies, and other tracking technologies. In many cases, this collection happens automatically.
  • Information you choose to publish, display, or transmit to other users or third parties is shared at your own risk. If you provide us with another person’s information, you must have their consent to do so.
  • Third-party advertisers may use cookies or other tracking technologies. We do not control those technologies or the way those parties collect and use information.

03

How we use your information

  • We use information to operate and improve the platform, facilitate transactions between users, understand user behaviour and preferences, and support our business activities.
  • We may use your information to contact you with service information, administrative notices, targeted advertisements, and other communications related to your use of the platform.
  • We do not publish, sell, or rent personal information to third parties for marketing purposes without your explicit consent. We may use information for analytics such as understanding usage patterns and audience size.
  • We may delete information that violates this policy or applicable law. Where we rely on your consent, you may withdraw it in writing, although withdrawal may affect our ability to provide services.

04

When information is disclosed

  • We may share information with our agents, employees, subsidiaries, affiliates, advertisers, and service providers who need it to operate and maintain the platform.
  • We may disclose information where required or directed by government, law enforcement, or regulatory authorities acting under applicable law.
  • If information must be transferred outside the relevant jurisdiction, we will do so in accordance with applicable data-protection requirements and seek comparable protection for the information transferred.

05

Security and retention

  • Information covered by this policy is maintained using secure servers and appropriate safeguards, including firewalls operated by Oxybricks.world or its service providers.
  • We retain information for no longer than necessary for the purposes for which it was processed, subject to legal and operational requirements.
  • Internet transmission is never completely risk-free. While we use reasonable efforts to protect your information, we cannot guarantee that security measures are completely foolproof or impenetrable.

06

Accessing and updating your information

  • You can change or update your information by accessing the platform with your user account. Subject to applicable law and reasonable conditions, you may also request erasure, restriction of processing, or transfer of your information.
  • We aim to respond to requests as soon as possible and no later than one month. Complex or numerous requests may take up to three months where permitted by law.
  • You may request changes or deletion of information you have submitted, but we may retain usage information where required or permitted by applicable law.

07

How to contact us

If you have questions about this policy, the information we hold about you, or your data-protection rights, please contact us.

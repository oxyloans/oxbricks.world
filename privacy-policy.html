01

Introduction and applicability

This Privacy Policy explains how Oxybricks.world collects, uses, processes, discloses, and maintains information in connection with your use of our platform.

This policy applies to information provided through the platform, including information you upload, submit, or provide to us through emails or other communications.

Where Oxybricks.world processes personal data, such processing will be carried out in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”) and applicable rules made thereunder.

This policy does not apply to information collected by third parties through the platform, including through links, advertisements, or other third-party services. Such third parties are responsible for their own privacy practices.

02

Information we collect

We may collect information directly from you or through third parties, including personally identifiable information, usage patterns, searches, interactions with advertisements or third-party links, and correspondence with us.

The categories of personal data collected may include identification and contact details, account information, transaction-related information, device and usage information, location information, and other personal data that is necessary for the services provided through the platform. The specific personal data collected will depend on the service or feature used.

We may automatically collect usage details such as your IP address, device information, browser information, location information, connections, cookies, tracking technologies, and other information relating to your use of the platform.

We collect and process personal data only for specified purposes communicated to you and in accordance with applicable law.

Information that you publish or transmit to other users or third parties through the platform is provided at your own risk. If you provide us with another person's information, you should do so only where you are permitted or authorised to do so under applicable law.

Third-party advertisers may use cookies, tracking technologies, or similar mechanisms. Oxybricks.world does not control the privacy practices of such third parties.

03

How we use your information

We use information to operate and improve the platform, facilitate transactions, understand user behaviour and preferences, and support our business activities.

We process personal data only for specified purposes, including providing and maintaining the platform and its services, facilitating transactions and communications, maintaining security, preventing fraud or misuse, improving our services, complying with legal and regulatory obligations, and other purposes specifically communicated to you.

We may use your personal data to contact you with service information, administrative notices, and other communications related to your use of the platform. Where processing for targeted advertising or other communications requires consent under applicable law, such consent will be obtained through a clear affirmative action.

We do not publish, sell, or rent personal data to third parties for their marketing purposes without consent where such consent is required under applicable law. We may use personal data for analytics and other purposes permitted by applicable law.

Where Oxybricks.world relies on consent for processing personal data, consent will be obtained through clear affirmative action after providing the applicable notice. Consent will be free, specific, informed, unconditional, and unambiguous. Where permitted under applicable law, you may withdraw consent through an available mechanism, with the ease of withdrawal being comparable to the ease with which consent was given.

Where consent is withdrawn, Oxybricks.world will cease processing based on that consent unless such processing is otherwise permitted or required under applicable law. Withdrawal of consent will not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

We may delete or restrict access to information that violates our policies or applicable law.

04

When information is disclosed

We may share information with agents, employees, subsidiaries, affiliates, advertisers, and service providers who require such information to operate, maintain, and support the platform.

Personal data will be disclosed or shared only for specified purposes and where permitted or required under applicable law, including where necessary to provide requested services through authorised service providers.

We may disclose personal data where required by applicable law or pursuant to a lawful order or direction of a competent authority.

If personal data is transferred outside India, such transfer will be carried out subject to applicable law, including any requirements or restrictions notified under applicable law.

05

Security and retention

We use reasonable security measures to protect information stored on our servers and systems, including appropriate technical and organisational safeguards. These systems may be operated by Oxybricks.world or authorised service providers.

Oxybricks.world will implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or other unauthorised processing, having regard to the nature of the personal data and the risks associated with its processing.

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the specified purpose for which it was processed and for such additional period as may be required or permitted by applicable law. Where the purpose for processing no longer exists and no legal or regulatory retention requirement applies, the personal data will be deleted or securely disposed of in accordance with applicable procedures.

In the event of a personal data breach, Oxybricks.world will take the measures required under applicable law, including applicable requirements relating to notification of the Data Protection Board and affected Data Principals, where applicable.

While we take reasonable measures to protect your personal data, no method of transmission or storage over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

06

Accessing and updating your information

You may change or update certain personal information through your account, subject to applicable law and reasonable verification requirements.

Subject to applicable law, you may exercise applicable rights as a Data Principal, including the right to access information about your personal data and its processing, request correction, completion, or updating of personal data, request erasure where applicable, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, and seek grievance redressal.

You may request correction, updating, or erasure of your personal data, subject to applicable law. Oxybricks.world may retain personal data where retention is required or permitted by applicable law or is necessary for the specified purpose for which it was collected.

Where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw your consent through the available mechanism. Withdrawal of consent will not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal and may affect our ability to provide services where the relevant processing is necessary for that service.

Oxybricks.world will provide appropriate means for Data Principals to exercise their applicable rights and raise grievances regarding the processing of their personal data.

Requests and grievances may be submitted using the contact details provided in the “How to contact us” section.
